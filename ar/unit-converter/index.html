❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How many inches are in a foot?

1 foot = 12 inches. 1 meter = 3.281 feet = 39.37 inches. 1 kilometer = 0.621 miles.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = (°C × 9÷5) + 32. Example: 30°C = (30 × 1.8) + 32 = 86°F. Reverse: °C = (°F − 32) × 5÷9.

How many kilograms are in a pound?

1 pound = 0.4536 kilogram. 1 kilogram = 2.205 pounds. 1 metric tonne = 1,000 kilograms = 2,205 pounds.

How do I convert kilometers to miles?

1 kilometer = 0.6214 miles. 1 mile = 1.6093 kilometers. Example: 100 km = 62.14 miles. Use the tool for instant conversion.

What is Kelvin and how does it differ from Celsius?

Kelvin is the absolute temperature scale used in science. Absolute zero (0 K) = −273.15°C and is the coldest possible temperature. To convert Celsius to Kelvin: add 273.15.

How many centimeters are in an inch?

1 inch = exactly 2.54 centimeters (an internationally defined exact figure). Example: a 15-inch screen = 38.1 cm diagonal.

What is the difference between metric tonne and US ton?

Metric tonne = 1,000 kilograms. US short ton = 907.18 kilograms. UK long ton = 1,016 kilograms. Internationally, "tonne" typically means metric.

How many millimeters are in an inch?

1 inch = exactly 25.4 millimeters. This is a precise international standard established by the 1959 international yard and pound agreement.

Most common conversions

  • Weight: 1 kg = 2.2046 lbs | 1 stone = 6.35 kg
  • Length: 1 mile = 1.6093 km | 1 foot = 30.48 cm | 1 inch = 2.54 cm
  • Temperature: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32 | 0°C = 32°F | 100°C = 212°F
  • Volume: 1 US gallon = 3.785 L | 1 UK gallon = 4.546 L

Metric vs. imperial around the world

Only three countries officially use the imperial system: the United States, Liberia and Myanmar. The UK uses a mix — metric officially but miles for road distances and stone/pounds for body weight in everyday use. The Middle East uses metric throughout.
