Key Facts for Indian Workers in Oman

FAQ — Indian Workers in Oman

Do Indian expats in Oman pay SPF deductions?

No. The Social Protection Fund (SPF) 8% deduction applies only to Omani nationals. Indian and other expatriate workers keep their full gross salary with no SPF deduction.

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated for Indians in Oman?

Under Article 61 of Oman Labour Law: 1 month basic salary per year of service, with partial years pro-rated. Example: 5 years at OMR 400 basic salary = OMR 2,000 gratuity. Use the End of Service Calculator for your exact figure.

Is there income tax on salary in Oman for Indian workers?

As of 2026, Oman does not impose personal income tax on salaries. A Personal Income Tax law was enacted in 2025 but is scheduled to take effect from 2028. Currently no tax deduction applies to Indian workers' salaries in Oman.

What is the best way to send money from Oman to India?

Popular services include: bank wire transfers (all Oman banks), exchange houses (Al Ansari, Mustaqbali, Western Union), and online platforms (Wise, Remitly). Compare rates and fees before sending. Use the OMR to INR Currency Converter to check the live rate.

What is the notice period in Oman?

Under Oman Labour Law, the notice period for indefinite contracts is typically one month, or as specified in the employment contract (which may be longer). Both employer and employee must give notice. Check your contract for the specific period that applies.
