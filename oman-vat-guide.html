How VAT is Charged in Oman

VAT-registered businesses add 5% to their prices at the point of sale. The VAT is collected on behalf of the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) and remitted quarterly or monthly depending on registration type.

FAQ

Do I pay VAT on my salary or rent in Oman?

No. Employee salaries are not subject to VAT. Residential rent is also VAT-exempt. You only pay VAT on purchases of goods and services at standard-rated businesses.

Is food zero-rated or 5% VAT in Oman?

Many basic food items (such as rice, bread, milk, eggs, vegetables, and fresh meat) may qualify for zero-rating at 0% VAT, though classification of specific items can vary. Processed food, restaurant meals, and fast food are generally subject to the standard 5% VAT. Verify with the Oman Tax Authority (taxoman.gov.om) for specific items.

Can tourists get VAT refunds in Oman?

Oman is developing a tourist VAT refund scheme. As of 2026, check with the Oman Tax Authority for the latest status on tourist VAT refund availability at airports and ports.
