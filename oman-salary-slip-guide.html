Why Basic Salary Matters Most

Your basic salary is the most important figure on your payslip for legal and financial purposes:

  • End-of-Service Gratuity: Calculated on basic salary only (not total salary)
  • Overtime Pay: Calculated as a % of basic hourly rate (125% weekday, 150% holiday)
  • SPF Contribution: Based on basic salary (Omanis only)
  • Bank Loans: Some banks use basic salary as the qualifying amount for loan limits
⚠️ If your employment contract shows a high basic salary but your payslip shows a lower basic with the rest called "allowances," this directly reduces your end-of-service entitlement. Make sure your contract and payslip match.
💡 WPS (Wage Protection System): All employers in Oman must pay salaries electronically via the Wage Protection System. Your payslip should always reflect what was actually transferred to your bank account. If there are discrepancies, you can file a complaint with the Ministry of Labour.

FAQ

My employer calls it "all-inclusive salary" — is that okay?

Some employers use a single "all-inclusive" salary figure with no breakdown. This is legal but be careful: for EOS calculation, the court may treat the entire all-inclusive amount as your basic salary. Get a written breakdown in your contract if possible.

My payslip shows less than my contract says — what can I do?

First raise the discrepancy in writing with your HR/employer. If unresolved, you can file a formal complaint with the Ministry of Labour (MOL). Keep copies of your employment contract, payslips, and bank statements as evidence. The MOL has dedicated dispute resolution services.

Is there income tax taken from my salary in Oman?

No. There is no personal income tax in Oman. Your payslip income tax line should always show OMR 0. See our Oman Tax Guide for full details.
