Key Facts for Pakistani Workers in Oman

💰

📋
End of Service — 1 Month per Year (Article 61) All expats are entitled to end-of-service gratuity: 1 month basic salary per year of service, with partial years pro-rated. Example: 5 years at OMR 350 basic = OMR 1,750.

FAQ — Pakistani Workers in Oman

Do Pakistani expats in Oman pay SPF deductions?

No. The SPF 8% deduction applies only to Omani nationals. Pakistani workers receive their full gross salary with no SPF deduction.

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated for Pakistanis in Oman?

Under Article 61: 1 month basic salary per year of service, partial years pro-rated. Example: 6 years at OMR 300 basic = OMR 1,800 gratuity. Use the End of Service Calculator for your exact figure.

Is there income tax on salary in Oman for Pakistani workers?

As of 2026, Oman does not impose personal income tax on salaries. PIT is expected from 2028. Currently no income tax applies to Pakistani workers' salaries in Oman.

How do I convert OMR to PKR?

Use the OMR to PKR Currency Converter on Adawati for the live rate. Always check the rate before transferring money. Exchange houses in Oman (Al Fardan, Western Union) typically offer better rates than banks for remittances.

What notice period is required when leaving a job in Oman?

Under Oman Labour Law, the notice period for indefinite contracts is typically one month, or as specified in your employment contract. Check your specific contract before resigning to avoid penalties.
