⚠️ The minimum wage of OMR 325 applies to Omani nationals only. Expatriate workers do not have a statutory national minimum wage. Their salary is determined by employment contract, market rates, and any sector-specific regulations.

What is the Wage Protection System (WPS)?

All employers in Oman must pay salaries through the Wage Protection System (WPS) — an electronic salary payment system monitored by the Ministry of Labour. Salaries must be paid via approved banks or exchange houses on or before the agreed payment date.

Employers who fail to pay salaries on time face:

  • Fines and penalties under the Labour Law
  • Suspension of new work permit issuance
  • Legal action if complaints are filed with the Ministry of Labour

If your employer delays your salary, you have the right to file a complaint at the Ministry of Labour offices or through their online portal.

Omani Nationalisation — Omanisation Quotas

Oman enforces Omanisation quotas requiring companies to employ a minimum percentage of Omani nationals depending on sector. The minimum wage law only applies to Omani employees covered by these quotas.

FAQ

Has the minimum wage in Oman increased recently?

The OMR 325 basic minimum wage has been in place since Ministerial Decision 294/2013. As of 2026, it has not been officially revised upward, though discussions about future adjustments continue. Check with the Ministry of Labour for the latest updates.

Can an employer pay an Omani employee less than OMR 325?

No. Paying an Omani national less than the statutory minimum (OMR 325 basic + OMR 100 housing = OMR 425 total) is a violation of Oman Labour Law. Employees can file complaints with the Ministry of Labour if this occurs.

Are domestic workers protected by Oman's labour law?

Domestic workers (household employees) in Oman are governed by a separate regulation — Ministerial Decision 189/2004. They are not covered by the main Labour Law in the same way. Their employment terms (salary, rest days, holidays) are specified in their individual contracts and the domestic worker regulations.
