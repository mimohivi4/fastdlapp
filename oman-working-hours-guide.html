How to Calculate Your Overtime Pay

Step 1 — Find your hourly rate:
Hourly rate = (Basic Salary ÷ 26 working days) ÷ 8 hours

Step 2 — Apply the overtime multiplier:
Normal overtime (daytime) = hourly rate × 1.25 × overtime hours
Night/rest day overtime = hourly rate × 1.50 × overtime hours

Example: Basic salary OMR 600/month. Worked 3 hours of daytime overtime.
Hourly rate = (600 ÷ 26) ÷ 8 = OMR 2.88/hour
Overtime pay = 2.88 × 1.25 × 3 = OMR 10.80

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard working hours in Oman?

8 hours per day and 45 hours per week under Article 68. During Ramadan, Muslim employees work a maximum of 6 hours per day (30 hours per week).

How is overtime calculated in Oman?

Daytime overtime (beyond 8 hours on a regular day) is paid at 125% of your normal hourly rate. Overtime after 9pm or on a rest day (Friday) is paid at 150%. The maximum overtime is 4 hours per day (total 12 hours in a day).

How many days of annual leave do I get in Oman?

30 calendar days per year after completing 1 year of service. In the first year, leave accrues at approximately 2.5 days per completed month of service.

Do non-Muslim expats get shorter hours during Ramadan?

The legal requirement for reduced hours (6 hours/day) applies specifically to Muslim employees. Non-Muslim expats typically continue their normal 8-hour schedule unless the employer voluntarily applies Ramadan hours to all staff. Check your employer's HR policy.

What happens to unused annual leave when I leave Oman?

Accrued but unused annual leave must be paid out upon termination. The payout is calculated based on your basic salary rate at the time of termination. This applies regardless of whether you resign or are terminated.
