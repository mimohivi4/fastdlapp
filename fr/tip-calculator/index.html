❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is the usual tip percentage?

In Western countries 15-20%. In the Gulf, tipping is not mandatory but 10-15% is common in hotel restaurants.

Is the tip calculated before or after VAT?

Usually on the pre-VAT price. Practices differ between countries and restaurants.

How do I split the bill equally?

Enter the total bill and tip percentage, then change the number of people. Each person's share including the tip will appear automatically.

Is tipping mandatory in Oman and the Gulf?

No, tipping is legally optional in Gulf countries. Some hotel restaurants automatically add a service charge (5-15%) to the bill — check before adding an additional tip.

What does "Service Charge" on the bill mean?

A service charge is a percentage (usually 5-15%) added automatically by the restaurant that goes to management — not necessarily directly to the server. If it appears on the bill, any additional tip is optional.

How do I calculate the tip for a large group?

Enter the total in the tool, choose a tip percentage (10-15% for Gulf), then enter the number of people. Each person's share including the tip will appear instantly.

When is a 20% tip recommended?

For excellent service, a celebratory meal, or when visiting the United States where servers' base salaries are very low and they depend mainly on tips.

Should I tip for delivery service?

Yes, 10% for delivery is common and appreciated. Delivery apps like Talabat and Careem allow adding it directly when ordering or upon receipt.

Tip Calculator — How Much to Tip Around the World

Tipping customs vary significantly by country. Enter the total bill, choose a tip percentage, and split by the number of diners. The calculator shows the tip per person and total per person so everyone knows exactly what to pay.

Tip rates by region

  • USA: 15-20% expected at sit-down restaurants, 10% for delivery
  • UK: 10-12.5% appreciated — check the bill first, many add a service charge automatically
  • Oman / UAE / Saudi Arabia: Not expected but appreciated; 5-10% is generous
  • Japan / South Korea: Tipping is considered rude — do not tip

Service charge vs. tip

A service charge added to your bill goes to the restaurant (and may or may not reach the server). A voluntary tip given directly to the server is different. In the UK, always check whether a service charge is already included before adding more.
