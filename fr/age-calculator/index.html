How many days has a 30 or 40-year-old lived?

A 30-year-old has lived approximately 10,950 days (262,800 hours). A 40-year-old approximately 14,610 days (350,640 hours). Enter your birthdate for your exact count.

What is the difference between my Hijri and Gregorian age?

The Hijri year is lunar (354-355 days), shorter than the Gregorian solar year by about 11 days. So your Hijri age will always be larger — for example, 30 Gregorian years ≈ 30.9 Hijri years.

Age Calculator — Exact Age in Years, Months and Days

This calculator gives your precise age down to the day — useful for passport applications, insurance forms, retirement planning, or simply satisfying curiosity. It handles leap years automatically and can calculate the difference between any two dates, not just your birthday to today.

Common uses

  • Checking legal age requirements (driving, voting, retirement)
  • Confirming age for visa and passport applications
  • Finding out how many days until an upcoming birthday
  • Verifying eligibility for age-restricted services

Leap year note

A leap year occurs every 4 years (with century exceptions). If you were born on February 29, the calculator handles this correctly — your birthday is treated as March 1 in non-leap years for counting purposes.
