About Us

The story of Adawati

Adawati is a collection of free and simple online tools, designed to help you complete calculations and daily tasks quickly and without complexity.

Our goal is to provide accurate, easy-to-use tools on any device while protecting your data privacy — most calculations run directly in your browser, and we disclose any external service we use.

The site is continuously developed, and we add new tools regularly based on user needs.
