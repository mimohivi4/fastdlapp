FAQ — Filipino OFWs in Oman

Do Filipino OFWs in Oman pay SPF deductions?

No. The Social Protection Fund (SPF) 8% deduction applies only to Omani nationals. Filipino OFWs keep their full gross salary with no SPF deduction from Oman. Note that SSS contributions in the Philippines are a separate obligation managed directly between you and SSS.

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated for Filipinos in Oman?

Under Article 61 of Oman Labour Law: 1 month basic salary per year of service, with partial years pro-rated. Example: 3 years at OMR 450 basic salary = OMR 1,350 gratuity. Use the End of Service Calculator for your exact figure.

Is there income tax on salary in Oman for Filipino workers?

As of 2026, Oman does not impose personal income tax on salaries. A law was enacted in 2025 but takes effect from 2028. Regarding Philippine tax: OFWs are generally exempt from Philippine income tax on their overseas earnings — consult BIR OFW guidelines for your specific situation.

What is the best way to send money from Oman to the Philippines?

Popular options include: Remitly, Western Union, bank transfers, and exchange houses. Compare rates and fees — the spread between rates can vary significantly. Use the OMR to PHP Currency Converter to see the live mid-market rate as a benchmark.

Does my POEA contract affect my gratuity in Oman?

Your POEA-standard employment contract and Oman Labour Law both provide protections. Oman Labour Law sets the minimum gratuity (Article 61), but your actual contract may offer better terms. If your contract provides higher gratuity or other benefits than the Oman law minimum, those terms apply. Always keep a copy of your contract.
