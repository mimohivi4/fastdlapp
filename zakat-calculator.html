How Zakat is calculated

Net zakatable assets = cash + gold + silver/investments − debts. If the result is above Nisab and one Hijri year has passed, Zakat = net × 2.5%.

Zakat = (Assets − Debts) × 0.025

Example: 8,000 − 500 = 7,500 → Zakat = 7,500 × 0.025 = 187.5.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as zakatable wealth?

Cash, bank balances, gold, silver, stocks, crypto held as investment, and business inventory. Your home, car, furniture and tools of trade are exempt.

Gold or silver Nisab — which to use?

Gold Nisab = 85g gold; silver Nisab = 595g silver. Silver Nisab is much lower, so more people qualify — many contemporary scholars prefer it. Enter your local equivalent in your currency above.

Can I pay Zakat in Ramadan?

Yes. You may pay early or align your Zakat year with Ramadan for simplicity, as long as Nisab was held for a lunar year.
