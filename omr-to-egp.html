How much is 1 OMR in Egyptian Pounds today?

Why is the EGP rate changing so much?

Egypt devalued the pound multiple times since 2022 as part of IMF-supported economic reforms. Since OMR is fixed to USD, every EGP devaluation means more Egyptian Pounds per OMR.
