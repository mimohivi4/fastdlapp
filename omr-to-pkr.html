❓ FAQ
Is 1 OMR more than 700 PKR?

In 2026, yes — 1 OMR is typically 720–760 PKR. The exact amount depends on the current USD/PKR rate since OMR is pegged to USD at 2.5973.

Why does the PKR rate change so much?

The Pakistani Rupee has experienced significant volatility due to economic factors. Since OMR is fixed to USD, every change in USD/PKR directly affects the OMR/PKR rate.

How much is 500 OMR in PKR?

500 OMR is approximately PKR 360,000–380,000 at 2026 rates. Use the live converter above for today's exact amount.
