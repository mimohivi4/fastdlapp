Can Expats Get a Loan in Oman?

Yes — most major banks in Oman offer personal loans to expatriate residents. Eligibility criteria and maximum loan amounts may differ from those available to Omani nationals, but expats in stable employment with salary transfer can access competitive rates.

💡 Key requirement: Most banks require your salary to be transferred to their account. This "salary assignment" is a standard condition and gives the bank automatic repayment security.
🚨 Important — YMYL Disclaimer: Loan regulations, debt service ratios, and interest rates in Oman are set by the Central Bank of Oman (CBO) and differ by product type, bank, and applicant profile. CBO Regulatory Framework — What You Should Know

The Central Bank of Oman (CBO) issues regulations that set debt service ratio limits and maximum tenors for personal lending. These limits vary depending on:

  • Loan product type — personal (non-housing) loans have different rules than housing loans
  • Borrower type — Omani nationals vs. expatriates may have different eligible tenors
  • Individual bank policies — banks set their own internal lending criteria within the CBO framework

The CBO framework generally sets maximum debt-service ratios and maximum tenors for each product category. For personal (non-housing) loans, maximum tenors under various CBO circulars can range from 5 to 10 years depending on product classification and bank approval. Always confirm the applicable rules with your lending bank.

Interest Rates — What to Expect

Personal loan interest rates in Oman are not subject to a universal regulated cap — rates are set by individual banks and finance companies within a competitive market. Rates depend on your salary level, employer type, loan amount, chosen bank, and whether you have a salary account there.

Required Documents for Expats

  • Valid passport + residence permit (iqama) — must be valid for 6+ months
  • Salary certificate / employment letter (recent, on company letterhead)
  • 3 months latest pay slips
  • 3–6 months bank statements
  • Proof of salary transfer to the bank
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from employer — some banks require this

FAQ

What happens to my loan if I leave Oman?

Your loan obligation remains even if you leave Oman. Banks may require you to settle the outstanding balance before they release your residence permit for cancellation. Defaulting can result in legal action in your home country through international enforcement channels.

Can I get a loan in Oman without salary transfer?

Very few banks offer loans without salary transfer to expatriates. Salary assignment is the primary security for the bank. Some may accept a post-dated cheque arrangement, but this is less common and rates are usually higher.

How long does loan approval take in Oman?

Personal loan approval typically takes 2–7 working days after submitting all required documents. Some banks offer pre-approved loans for existing salary account holders with approval in 24–48 hours.
