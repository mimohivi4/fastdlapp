Work Visa Process — Step by Step

1
Job offer & contract
Receive a signed employment contract from your Oman employer. Review salary, allowances, working hours, and notice period before signing.
2
Employer applies for work permit
Your employer submits a work permit application to Oman's Ministry of Labour (Manpower) including your documents: passport copy, educational certificates, medical certificate.
3
Employment visa issued
Once the work permit is approved, the employment visa is stamped in your passport at the Oman embassy/consulate in your country. You then travel to Oman.
4
Residence card (IQama) registration
Within 30 days of arrival, your employer applies for your residence card at the Royal Oman Police. You will need to complete medical tests (HIV, TB) at an approved clinic.
5
IQama card collected
Your residence card (IQama) is issued. Keep it safe — you need it for banking, SIM registration, rental agreements and most official transactions in Oman.

Family Visa Requirements

Minimum salary requirement: Typically OMR 300–400/month (varies by nationality and employer)
Accommodation: Proof of suitable housing (lease agreement)
Documents needed: Marriage certificate (attested), children's birth certificates (attested), sponsor's IQama, sponsor's salary certificate
Note: Requirements can vary — confirm with Royal Oman Police or your employer's PRO (Public Relations Officer).

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get an Oman work visa?

The processing time varies. Once your employer submits the work permit, approval typically takes 2–4 weeks. The embassy visa stamp in your home country can take an additional 1–2 weeks. Total timeline from job offer to arrival in Oman: 4–8 weeks typically.

Can I change jobs in Oman without a NOC?

Oman has significantly reformed its labour mobility rules. In many cases you can change employers without a No-Objection Certificate, particularly after completing your contract term. The Royal Oman Police website has the current rules. Always check the latest regulations before assuming you can transfer freely.

What documents do I need to attest for Oman?

Typically: educational certificates (degree, diploma), marriage certificate (if bringing family), birth certificates of children. Documents must be notarized in your home country, then attested by the Foreign Ministry, then verified by the Oman embassy. Your employer's PRO will guide you on what specific documents are required.

Do I need exit re-entry permission to travel from Oman?

No — exit re-entry visas have been abolished for most expats. You can leave and return freely during the validity of your residence permit. Ensure your IQama is valid before traveling. Some employers may have internal HR processes to follow; check with your company's HR department.
