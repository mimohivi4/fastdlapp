🇦🇪 UAE VAT — Key Facts

The UAE introduced VAT on 1 January 2018 at a standard rate of 5% — one of the lowest VAT rates in the world. It applies across all seven Emirates.

Zero-rated in the UAE (0% VAT):

  • Exports of goods and services outside GCC
  • International transportation (air, sea, land)
  • Certain food items (basic unprocessed foods)
  • Healthcare and educational services
  • New residential buildings (first supply)

Regulatory authority: Federal Tax Authority — tax.gov.ae | Last updated: August 2026

UAE VAT Calculator — Add or Remove 5% Tax

The UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) applies a 5% VAT rate on most goods and services across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain. This calculator handles both adding VAT to a net price and extracting VAT from a gross (VAT-inclusive) price.

Adding VAT: formula

Total = Net price × 1.05  |  VAT amount = Net price × 0.05

Removing VAT: formula

Net price = Gross price ÷ 1.05  |  VAT amount = Gross price − Net price. Use this when a quoted price already includes VAT and you need the pre-tax figure for accounting.
