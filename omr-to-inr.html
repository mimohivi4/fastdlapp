❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Is Omani Rial stronger than Indian Rupee?

Yes. 1 OMR = approximately 210–220 INR. The OMR is pegged to USD at a fixed rate of 1 OMR = 2.5973 USD, making it one of the strongest currencies globally.

Does the OMR rate change daily?

OMR is fixed to USD (never changes). But INR floats, so the OMR/INR rate changes every day based on how INR performs against the dollar.

How much is 100 OMR in Indian Rupees?

100 OMR is approximately INR 21,000–22,000. Use the converter above for the exact live rate today.

What is the best exchange rate for OMR to INR?

Exchange houses in Oman (Al Ansari, Lulu Exchange, Musandam) typically offer rates close to the interbank rate. Online services like Wise often have the best mid-market rate with transparent fees.
