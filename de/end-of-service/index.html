📖 End of Service Gratuity — Updated Omani Labor Law

Article 61 of the Updated Oman Labour Law:
Workers not covered by the Social Protection Law are entitled to a gratuity of no less than one full basic monthly salary per year of service, with partial years calculated proportionally.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Source: Article 61, Omani Labor Law | Content last updated: August 2026

What is the difference between the new and old system?

Old system: 15 days for years 1–3, 20 days for years 3–5, 30 days after 5 years (calculated from daily rate = basic÷26). New system (Article 61): minimum is one full month's salary per year with fractions pro-rated. The higher amount applies per the employment contract and applicable system.

Am I entitled to gratuity if I worked less than one year?

Under the updated Omani Labor Law (Article 61), fractions of a year are calculated proportionally. An employee who completed 6 months is entitled to 0.5 months' salary.

Is an expatriate entitled to end of service gratuity?

Yes. Oman's Labor Law applies to both Omani nationals and expatriates. Expatriate employees are entitled to end of service gratuity under the stated provisions, and also have an SPF savings program funded by the employer.

Are allowances included in the gratuity calculation?

Under labor law, gratuity is calculated on basic salary only. Some companies include allowances in the individual contract and use them in the calculation — check your contract to confirm.

What is the difference in end of service between Omani nationals and expatriates?

For Omanis: end of service is managed through the SPF savings program with employer contributions of 11.5% monthly. For expatriates: calculated under Article 61 (minimum one month's salary per year of service).

When must end of service gratuity be paid?

Under Article 91 of Oman's Labor Law, the employer must pay the employee's wages and all entitlements upon termination of employment. If the employee leaves voluntarily, entitlements must be paid within 7 days of the leaving date. In case of delay, the employee can file a complaint with the Ministry of Labour.

Is gratuity paid in case of resignation?

Entitlements differ based on the reason for termination, the applicable legal system, and the employment contract. Specific circumstances may affect the entitlement. Check your contract and consult a specialist lawyer to confirm.

What is the minimum service period to claim gratuity?

End of service gratuity is calculated per the provisions applicable to the employee, their contract, and the prevailing legal system. Do not use the 3-year rule as a general rule; entitlements may differ based on the termination reason and applicable system.

Am I entitled to full gratuity if I resign?

Under the updated Article 61, gratuity is calculated proportionally for each year of service whether termination is employee- or employer-initiated. In case of disciplinary dismissal under Article 40, gratuity may be forfeited in whole or in part. Consult a specialist lawyer for your specific situation.

How is gratuity calculated for a partial year?

Under Article 61, fractions are pro-rated: 6 months = 0.5 month of basic salary. Example: expat employee with OMR 400 basic salary and 5 years and 6 months service: 400 × 5.5 = OMR 2,200.

Oman End of Service Gratuity — How It's Calculated

Under Oman's Labour Law (Article 61, Royal Decree 53/2023), the minimum gratuity is one full month's basic salary for each year of service, with partial years calculated on a pro-rata basis. Omani nationals covered by the Social Protection Fund (SPF) have contributions managed through that programme instead.

Key factors affecting the calculation

  • Gratuity is based on basic salary only — housing allowance, transport and other benefits are excluded unless the employment contract specifies otherwise
  • Partial years are pro-rated — 6 months of service = 0.5 months' gratuity
  • Omani nationals enrolled in SPF may have different entitlements — check with your employer or the SPF directly
