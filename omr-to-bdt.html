How much is 1 OMR in Bangladeshi Taka?

In 2026, 1 OMR is approximately 280–300 BDT. The exact rate changes daily with USD/BDT movements.

Is the Bangladesh Taka (BDT) a stable currency?

The BDT is managed by Bangladesh Bank and has experienced moderate depreciation over the years. The OMR/BDT rate can shift when Bangladesh Bank adjusts its exchange rate policy.
