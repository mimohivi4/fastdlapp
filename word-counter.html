❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How many words fit on one A4 page?

An A4 page at 12pt font with standard margins holds approximately 400–500 words. Double spacing or wide margins reduce that number.

How many minutes to read 1000 words?

The average reader reads 200–250 words per minute. 1,000 words takes about 4–5 minutes. Technical texts may take longer.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes — the tool supports Arabic, English and mixed text. Words are counted by spaces regardless of language.

How many words should an SEO article contain?

Successful SEO articles are typically 1,200–2,500 words. Longer articles rank better in many cases, but quality and comprehensive answers matter more than quantity.

What are character limits on social platforms?

Twitter/X: 280 characters. Instagram caption: 2,200 characters. LinkedIn post: 3,000 characters. WhatsApp status: 700 characters.

How many characters in one SMS message?

160 Latin characters or 70 Arabic characters per SMS. Anything over that is split into multiple messages and may be charged separately.

Do numbers count as words in the tool?

Yes — any sequence of characters surrounded by spaces counts as one word. The number "2024" counts as one word.

What is the difference between words and symbols in academic writing?

Most universities count words, not characters. Some exclude reference lists or tables from the word count. Always check your institution's guidelines.

Word Counter — Why Word Count Matters

Whether you're writing a university essay, a social media post or a blog article, word count affects how your content is received and ranked. This counter gives you words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs in real time as you type or paste.

Platform character limits

  • X (Twitter): 280 characters per post
  • Instagram caption: 2,200 characters (first 125 shown without "more")
  • Meta description (SEO): 155–160 characters recommended
  • LinkedIn post: 3,000 characters

Reading time estimate

The average adult reads 200-250 words per minute. A 1,000-word article takes about 4-5 minutes to read. A 2,500-word blog post takes 10-12 minutes — enough depth for SEO purposes without losing reader attention on most topics.
