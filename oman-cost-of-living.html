Frequently Asked Questions

Is Oman expensive compared to the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

Oman is generally 10–20% less expensive than Dubai (UAE) for accommodation and dining. Compared to Riyadh (Saudi Arabia), costs are broadly similar. Muscat is one of the most affordable Gulf capitals for expats.

Is a housing allowance common in Oman?

Yes. Most professional expat packages in Oman include a housing allowance, typically OMR 100–300/month depending on company and seniority. Some employers provide free accommodation. Always check whether the advertised salary is basic only or inclusive of allowances.

How much should I earn to live comfortably in Muscat?

For a single expat: OMR 600–800/month total compensation is sufficient for a modest but comfortable lifestyle. For a family of 4 without school allowance: OMR 1,500–2,000+. Oil & gas and professional roles typically pay well above this — use the Salary Calculator to check your specific net take-home.

Are utilities included in Oman rent?

Rarely. Most Muscat apartments are rented excluding utilities. Electricity and water are billed separately by NAAM (formerly Muscat Electricity Distribution Company). Electricity costs spike in summer (June–September) due to air conditioning demand — budget OMR 40–80/month during those months.
