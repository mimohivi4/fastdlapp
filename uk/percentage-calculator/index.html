UK Percentage Calculator — VAT, Discounts & Service Charge

In the UK, percentages come up constantly: 20% standard VAT on most purchases, 12.5% optional service charge at restaurants, and seasonal discounts from 10–70%. This calculator handles three modes — finding X% of an amount, finding what percentage X is of Y, and calculating percentage change.

UK VAT — add and remove

  • Add 20% VAT: Net × 1.20  |  Example: £500 → £600
  • Remove 20% VAT: Gross ÷ 1.20  |  Example: £600 → £500

Service charge vs. tip

UK restaurants often add a 12.5% optional service charge. Unlike the US, tipping culture is not compulsory. If service charge is already on the bill, no additional tip is expected. You can legally request removal of the service charge.
