How to Calculate 20% UK VAT

The standard UK VAT rate is 20%. To add VAT: multiply the net price by 1.20. To remove VAT from a gross price: divide by 1.20. Use the percentage calculator above — enter 20 as the percentage and your price as the number, and it shows the VAT amount instantly. Note: the site's dedicated VAT calculator is for Gulf countries (5%/15%); for UK VAT, the percentage calculator is the right tool.

Stone & Pounds to Kilograms

The unit converter handles stone-to-kg conversion. Since the BMI calculator uses metric (kg and cm), convert first: enter your weight in pounds in the unit converter (Weight section) to get kg. 1 stone = 14 lbs = 6.35 kg.

GBP Currency Converter

Convert GBP to USD, EUR, AED, INR, CAD and 25+ more with rates updated every 4 hours from an external live-rates API. The AED/USD against GBP is useful for UK expats sending money to the Gulf.
